## About

I study how intelligent systems perceive, reason, and act through a physical body. My work and open-source projects connect robot-learning foundations with practical pipelines for manipulation, retargeting, simulation, and reproducible evaluation.

I am particularly interested in:

- transferable visuomotor policies for embodied agents;
- human-to-robot hand retargeting across different morphologies;
- simulation, benchmarks, and tooling that make robotics research easier to reproduce.

## Research Approach

```text
Perception  →  Representation  →  Policy  →  Control  →  Evaluation
                    simulation ↔ real-world feedback
```

- **Build from first principles.** Make assumptions, interfaces, and system boundaries explicit.
- **Evaluate with evidence.** Separate runnable demonstrations from validated performance claims.
- **Share reproducible paths.** Turn research notes into code, documentation, and reusable workflows.
